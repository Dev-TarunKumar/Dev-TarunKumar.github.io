## Brand & Style
This design system sets the foundation for Tarun’s personal digital home—a platform straddling AI engineering, systems-building, linguistic exploration, and perpetual learning. The aesthetic is clean, energetic, optimistic, and deeply human. Rather than the cold, dark, machine-like tropes common in AI engineering portfolios, the design leans into warmth, airiness, and intellectual vibrancy.

The interface communicates:
- **Intellectual Curiosity & Rigor:** High typographic discipline, ample whitespace, and structured data modules mirroring engineering precision.
- **Warmth & Global Perspective:** Gentle off-white canvases, bilingual glyph accents (Kanji/Hanzi), and vibrant, daylight-toned interactions.
- **Approachability:** Soft tactile surfaces, pill elements, and open geometry that invite reading, testing demos, and conversation.

## Colors
The palette balances an ultra-clean, daylight-charged foundation with decisive, high-chroma accents:

- **Primary (`#FF5A36` - Kinetic Coral):** Drives the primary calls to action, active states, key interactive indicators, and primary visual emphasis. Communicates energy and builder momentum.
- **Secondary (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Represents deep technical systems, AI logic, and structural focus. Used for code block highlights, secondary action links, and specialized badge states.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Sky Mist):** A luminous accent applied to language acquisition tags, progress markers, subtle tint fills, and decorative gradients.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate):** Anchors typography with high readability without the harshness of pure black. Paired with secondary text tints in slate-500 (`#64748B`).
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Primary background is `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Warmth) to avoid ocular fatigue, elevated by pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces and interactive containers.

## Typography
Typography is paired to deliver instant warmth in display positions and effortless reading stamina across technical writing and project deep-dives.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines, Subheads, Labels):** Offers geometric clarity with friendly humanist terminals. Used for all primary titling, metadata chips, and button labels. Tight tracking applied at large scales creates an authoritative, modern presence.
- **Inter (Body Text):** Used exclusively for long-form essays, research breakdowns, and UI commentary. Neutral, ultra-legible, and optimized for screen rendering at various densities.
- **JetBrains Mono (Code & Technical Metas):** Handles inline code snippets, mathematical notation, model parameters, and vocabulary flashcard metadata.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a flexible 12-column grid system bounded by generous dynamic whitespace. Content breathes freely, emphasizing an airy, uncluttered feel.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margin, vertical stacking for all feature cards and profile sidebars.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margin, dual-column project and article grids.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width constrained to `1200px` (or `760px` for prose/reading views), `3rem` margins.
- **Rhythm:** Macro vertical spacing between portfolio sections utilizes `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` to preserve breathing room, while component interiors maintain a compact, deliberate density using `space-sm` and `space-md`.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through subtle multi-layered ambient diffusion and surface tonal shifts rather than heavy structural borders or stark drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Soft, warm off-white (`#FAF9F6`).
  - **Tier 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border using `#1E293B` at 6% opacity (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`).
  - **Tier 2 (Elevated Overlays & Flyouts):** Pure white with ambient diffused elevation: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Hover Micro-elevation:** Cards translate -2px along the Y-axis on hover, while their shadow blooms softly (`0 20px 40px -12px rgba(255, 90, 54, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`), incorporating a gentle warm primary bloom.
- **Glass Accents:** Floating navigation and media badges use backdrop-filter (`blur(12px)`) over 80% opacity white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).

## Shapes
A pill-forward, organic geometry anchors the friendly identity of the site. Corners are generous, smooth, and friendly:

- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Applied uniformly to interactive tags, language proficiency chips, filter badges, and primary pill buttons.
- **Cards & Interactive Containers (`rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem to `rounded-3xl` / 2rem):** Used for project showcase blocks, blog feature entries, and profile callouts.
- **Small Elements (`rounded-lg` / 0.75rem):** Applied to form fields, code preview windows, and icon badge containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), `#FF5A36` background with white text, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. Subtle warm hover glow. No harsh border.
- **Secondary:** White or light canvas background, `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`, `#1E293B` text. Transitions to electric indigo text and outline on focus/hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, `#64748B` text, padding `space-sm` `space-md`, shifting to `#FF5A36` with a soft coral-tinted hover backdrop (`rgba(255, 90, 54, 0.06)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Tags:** Height `28px` to `32px`, pill radius, uppercase or capitalized `label-sm` tracking.
- **Language Chips:** Distinctive Sky/Indigo tinted surfaces (e.g., `#E0F2FE` background with `#0369A1` text for Japanese JLPT/Kanji updates, and `#EEF2FF` with `#4338CA` text for HSK/Chinese notes). Includes bilingual character prefixes (e.g., 「日本語」 or 「中文」).
- **Tech Stack Badges:** Monochromatic deep slate text on subtle cool grey-tinted pills (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`).

### Cards
- **Project & Blog Cards:** Pure white surface, `rounded-3xl`, padding `space-lg` to `space-xl`. Enclosed by an ultra-subtle hairline border (`1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`). Includes top metadata slot (date/tag) and bottom interactive hover affordance.
- **Interactive Demos / Lab Snippets:** Embedded sandbox cards styled with a contrasting dark slate header bar (`#0F172A`) containing window actions, resting on an airy container body.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- Fully rounded (`rounded-xl` or full pill for simple single-row inputs), `#FFFFFF` surface, border `1.5px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.1)`. Focus state drops a clear 3px focus ring tinted in Coral at 20% opacity (`rgba(255, 90, 54, 0.2)`).

### Language Study Flashcard / Vocab Callout
- Bespoke micro-component for language milestones: soft ivory surface (`#FFFDFB`), subtle orange or indigo left-accent line (3px), pairing high-contrast kanji/hanzi display with clean romaji/pinyin and definition in `Inter`.
